# Privacy Policy – Fit Companion Effective Date: November 21, 2026 Application: Fit Companion Developer: Arpit Paul Contact Email: arpitpaul30@gmail.com ## 1. Introduction Welcome to Fit Companion. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Fit Companion handles information when you use our application. Fit Companion is a workout organization and fitness companion application designed to help users create, organize, and follow personalized workout routines. We believe your fitness information should remain private. Fit Companion is designed to operate with local data storage and does not require an account or registration. ## 2. Information We Collect Fit Companion does not collect or transmit personal information to developer-operated servers. The application stores certain information locally on your device, including: - Workout routines and weekly schedules. - Exercise names, sets, repetitions, and other workout details. - Workout preferences and application settings. - Information you voluntarily enter into the application. This information remains on your device and is used exclusively to provide the application's functionality. We do not operate a cloud database or user-account service for storing your workout information. ## 3. How Your Information Is Used Information stored within Fit Companion is used to: - Organize and display your workout routines. - Display exercises scheduled for specific days. - Help you follow personalized workout plans. - Save your application preferences. - Provide workout reminders and notifications, where enabled. We do not sell, rent, or share your workout information with advertisers or data brokers. ## 4. Data Storage and Security All workout information and application preferences are stored locally on your device. Fit Companion does not upload your workout routines or personal workout information to external servers. Since your information is stored locally, its security also depends on your device's security settings and protections. If you uninstall the application or clear its storage, locally stored information may be permanently deleted. Fit Companion does not currently provide cloud backup or cloud restore functionality. ## 5. Internet Access Fit Companion may require an internet connection for certain platform-related functionality, application distribution, or Google Play services. However, the application does not use an internet connection to upload locally stored workout information to developer-operated servers. ## 6. Advertisements and Analytics Fit Companion does not display advertisements. The application does not integrate advertising networks, advertising trackers, analytics services, or third-party crash-reporting services. We do not track users' activity across applications or websites. ## 7. Third-Party Services Fit Companion may rely on Google Play Services and Google Play Billing for platform-related functionality and payment processing, where applicable. These services are operated independently by Google and are governed by Google's own privacy policies and terms. Fit Companion does not collect or store users' payment card information. For further information, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy ## 8. Permissions and Notifications Fit Companion may request notification permissions to provide workout reminders and other relevant notifications. These notifications are intended to help users follow their workout schedules. You can manage or disable notification permissions through your Android device settings. The application uses permissions only where necessary to provide its intended functionality. ## 9. Data Sharing Fit Companion does not sell, rent, trade, or share users' locally stored workout information with third parties. We do not maintain a developer-operated database containing users' personal or workout information. Third-party platform services, such as Google Play Billing, may independently process information necessary to provide their services. ## 10. Children's Privacy Fit Companion does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The application does not require users to create accounts or submit personal information to access its core workout organization features. If you believe personal information has been inadvertently provided to us, please contact us using the details below. ## 11. Data Retention and Deletion Your workout information remains stored locally on your device until you delete it or remove the application's stored data. You can generally delete locally stored information by: - Removing individual workout information within the application, where supported. - Clearing Fit Companion's application storage through Android settings. - Uninstalling the application. Fit Companion does not maintain cloud backups of your workout information. ## 12. Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy when application features, functionality, or applicable requirements change. Any updated version will be published at the same privacy policy URL, with a revised effective date where appropriate. We encourage users to review this page periodically. ## 13.
